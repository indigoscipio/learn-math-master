proof that if pos num
x and y are inversely proportional and
y and z are inverely proportional
then x and z are directly proportional

answer:
x and y are inversely proportional ->
x . y = k1
y = k1/x

y and z are inverely proportional
y . z = k2 -> subst y
y = k2/z

therefore 
k1/x = k2/z
k1 . z = k2 . x -> divide by z on both side
k1 = (k2 . x) / z
k1 = k2 . x / z
divide both sides by k2
k1 / k2 = x / z

left side -> k1/k2 is constant number / constant number k
right side is quotient x/z

since x/z = k, the ratio of x/z is constant
